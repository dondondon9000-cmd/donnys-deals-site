Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Donny's Deals is a one-person resale shop. This page explains what information is collected across the Donny's Deals storefront (this site) and the internal tools used to run the shop, and how it's used.

What this site collects

This storefront page itself doesn't have accounts, logins, or forms. It displays publicly listed items for sale and links out to Donny's Deals' stores on eBay, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted, and Whatnot. No personal information is collected from visitors browsing this page.

Social media connections

Donny's Deals uses official platform APIs (Meta's Graph API for Facebook and Instagram, and TikTok's Content Posting API) to publish promotional videos to its own Facebook Page, Instagram account, and TikTok account. These connections are authorized directly by the shop owner through each platform's own login and consent screen, and are used solely to post content — never to read, collect, or share any other user's personal data from those platforms.

What's never collected or stored

Donny's Deals does not collect payment information, passwords, or private messages through this site. Purchases happen entirely on the third-party marketplaces linked above (eBay, Depop, etc.), each of which has its own privacy policy governing that transaction.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to donnysdeals.ai@gmail.com.
